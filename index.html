Fabric Fitting Room

See your furniture in a new fabric before you reupholster. Everything runs on this Mac.

The app's engine is not running. In Terminal, run bash run.sh in this folder, then open http://127.0.0.1:8765.

1. Furniture photo

Drop a photo of the couch, sofa or chair here, or click to choose

2. Fabric samples

Drop one or more fabric photos, or click to choose

Flat, evenly lit close-ups work best. You get one render per sample.

3. Options

The first render loads the model, and the very first one downloads it. Watch Terminal for download progress.

Renders

Renders will appear here

  1. Add one photo of the furniture. A clear, front-on shot in good light works best.
  2. Add a photo of each fabric or material you are considering.
  3. Press the button. Each fabric gets its own render, one after another.

Pinch or Cmd/Ctrl-scroll to zoom; scroll, or drag, to move around.
